# Cancellation & Refund Policy

_Last updated: August 30, 2026_

## 1. Right of withdrawal and changes

You may withdraw from booked services by notifying VALLARTA MÁGICO's official contact numbers. Any request is valid only once we confirm receipt.

## 2. Cancellation and Refund Criteria

- **24 hours notice (or more):** 100% refund of the amount paid for the booked service.
- **Between 12 and 24 hours notice:** 50% refund of the amount paid.
- **Less than 12 hours notice:** No refund applies. The full amount will be considered an operational and schedule-blocking expense.

## 3. No-Shows and Late Arrivals

A No Show is considered when the customer does not show up at the established date, time and meeting point. In the event of a No Show or a late arrival that prevents the activity from starting on time, there is no right to a full or partial refund. VALLARTA MÁGICO is not obligated to reschedule or modify the activity for users who arrive after the established time.
