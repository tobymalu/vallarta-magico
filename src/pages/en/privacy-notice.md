# Privacy Notice

_Last updated: September 2026_

## 1. Identity and commitment of the Data Controller

At VALLARTA MÁGICO, we deeply value your trust and privacy. We're committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website and book our experiences.

## 2. Personal data we collect

To provide our services, we may ask for the following information:

- Full name.
- Contact information (email and phone/WhatsApp number).
- Billing or payment details (processed securely and encrypted).
- Information relevant to your safety on tours (age, medical restrictions or allergies, if the activity requires it).

## 3. What do we use your information for? (Purpose)

All the information you provide is used exclusively and strictly to deliver our services. The main purposes are:

- Processing, confirming and managing your tour and activity bookings.
- Sending you tickets, itineraries and meeting points.
- Contacting you in case of schedule changes, weather cancellations or emergencies.
- Providing customer support before, during and after your trip.

## 4. Zero sale of data to third parties (our commitment)

At VALLARTA MÁGICO we have a strict policy: we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.

Your data will only be shared with the tours' final operators (captains, guides or parks) to the extent strictly necessary to allow your access to the booked activity (for example, providing your name for a boat's boarding list).

## 5. Protection and Security

We implement administrative, technical and physical security measures designed to protect your personal data against damage, loss, alteration, destruction, or unauthorized use, access or processing.

## 6. Your Data Protection Rights

You own your information. At any time, you have the right to access the data we hold, correct it if inaccurate, request its deletion from our database, or object to its use.

To exercise these rights, simply send a request to our official email address or contact our customer service WhatsApp number.

## 7. Changes to this Privacy Notice

VALLARTA MÁGICO reserves the right to make changes to this privacy notice at any time to comply with legislative updates or new requirements for providing our services. Any changes will be published on this same page.
