**Los Veranos Canopy** is Puerto Vallarta's largest and most famous zip-line park. Its main draw is an **18 zip-line circuit over the river and jungle**, paired with a transfer by **APEX speedboat** that crosses Banderas Bay and skips the highway traffic. It also includes access to an animal sanctuary, river slides and an artisanal tequila tasting.

## Why is Los Veranos Canopy the best zip-line park in Vallarta?

If you're after adrenaline paired with mountain-meets-beach scenery, this tour beats the traditional circuits for three key reasons:

1. **You arrive by sea (no traffic).** Skip the tedious highway drive. Your excursion starts aboard a speedboat crossing the bay from Puerto Vallarta or Nuevo Vallarta.
2. **The longest circuit around.** 18 zip-lines let you fly more than 30 meters above the trees and the Horcones River.
3. **Companion Pass.** If someone in your group doesn't want to fly because of a fear of heights, they can get the boat ride and enjoy the river-side restaurant, the natural pools, the kayaks and the exotic sanctuary.

## Departure points and schedule

The tour has two daily departures: **8:45 AM** and **10:45 AM** (arrive 30 minutes early for check-in):

- **Puerto Vallarta:** Chedraui Playa de Oro parking lot (North Hotel Zone).
- **Nuevo Vallarta:** Pier "E", Paradise Village (next to Hospital Joya).

## Useful tips and safety

- **Required footwear:** wear sneakers or closed shoes with a strap that won't fly off. Huaraches or sandals aren't allowed on the circuit.
- **Maximum weight:** the maximum weight allowed per harness is 129 kg (285 lbs).
- **Restrictions:** not allowed for pregnant women, people with recent surgeries, back or neck conditions, or under the influence of alcohol.
- **What to pack:** swimsuit, towel, clothes you don't mind getting wet, biodegradable insect repellent and cash for food and tips.
