Getting to **Colomitos Beach** (known as the smallest beach in Mexico) can be done two ways: **walking 30 to 40 minutes** from Boca de Tomatlán along a moderately difficult jungle trail, or **by water taxi / boat in just 5 to 10 minutes**. If you're after adventure and exercise, go by land; if you want comfort, are traveling with kids, or bringing coolers and gear, the boat is the way to go.

## Option 1: getting to Colomitos by land (hike)

The trail connecting Boca de Tomatlán with Colomitos is one of the most popular ecotourism routes in Puerto Vallarta.

### What's the route like, step by step?

1. Catch a "Boca de Tomatlán" city bus in the Romantic Zone (corner of Basilio Badillo and Camichín).
2. Once in Boca de Tomatlán, cross the pedestrian bridge over the river and follow the signs along the coastline.
3. The trail mixes stone steps, wooden bridges, climbs among tree roots, and tropical vegetation.

- **Duration:** 30 to 45 minutes (one way).
- **Difficulty level:** moderate.
- **Cost:** free (only the transport to Boca de Tomatlán, approx. $10 - $20 MXN).

> **Best for:** hiking lovers, younger travelers, and visitors in good physical shape.

## Option 2: getting to Colomitos by boat or water taxi

If you'd rather save your energy, or plan to continue on to other southern beaches (like Las Ánimas, Majahuitas or Yelapa), going by sea is perfect.

### Boat options

- **Water taxi (local panga):** these leave constantly from the Boca de Tomatlán pier or from Los Muertos Beach downtown.
- **Organized boat tours:** excursions that combine Colomitos with snorkeling at Los Arcos and a stop at Majahuitas or Las Ánimas, with food and drinks included.

- **Travel time:** 5 to 10 minutes from Boca de Tomatlán (or 30 minutes from Los Muertos).
- **Estimated cost:** $100 to $150 MXN per person by panga from Boca de Tomatlán (one way).

> **Best for:** families with kids, older adults, or anyone bringing a cooler, umbrellas and beach gear.

## Comparison chart: which should you choose?

| Criteria | By land (hike) | By boat / water taxi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Travel time | 35 - 45 minutes | 5 - 10 minutes |
| Physical effort | High (climbs, heat, humidity) | None (just enjoy the view) |
| Cost | Free | $100 - $150+ MXN |
| Recommended gear | Light backpack and sneakers | Coolers, umbrellas, toys |
| Views | Panoramic, from the jungle and cliffs | Coastal perspective, from the sea |

## Local tips for your visit to Colomitos

- **Go early:** the beach is small and the sand fills up fast from noon on.
- **Bring your own supplies:** Colomitos doesn't have much fixed restaurant infrastructure on the sand; bring water and snacks (and take all your trash back with you).
- **Combine both experiences:** a great strategy is hiking in to Colomitos and taking a water taxi back, or to continue on to Las Ánimas.
