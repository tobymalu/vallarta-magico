## Safety tips for your trip

- **Save 911.** It works even from foreign mobile phones roaming internationally in Mexico.
- **Keep your travel insurance handy.** Always have a digital copy of your medical insurance policy: private hospitals in Mexico may ask for a credit-card deposit before admitting you.
- **Share your location.** If you're heading out on your own (outside a guided tour), share your live location with someone you trust.
- **Save this page.** Add it to your home screen or send it to yourself on WhatsApp before heading out — that way you have it even if your internet connection gets spotty.
