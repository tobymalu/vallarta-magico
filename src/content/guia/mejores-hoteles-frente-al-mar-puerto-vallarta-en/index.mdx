Puerto Vallarta has beachfront options for every budget: from the vibrant Romantic Zone to the exclusivity of the North Hotel Zone and the calm of the South Side. Here's our local team's curated pick to plan your 2026 and 2027 trip — and if you want to pair your stay with the best of the city, don't miss [our Malecón guide](/en/guide/actividades/el-malecon-puerto-vallarta) either.

## Why choose a beachfront hotel in Puerto Vallarta?

Staying right on the beach doesn't just guarantee panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean and spectacular sunsets — it also gives you direct, hassle-free access to the water activities and boat trips that leave right from the same bay.
