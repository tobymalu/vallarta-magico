The Cerro de la Cruz Viewpoint offers the best 360° panoramic view of Puerto Vallarta and Banderas Bay. Admission is **100% free** and it's open 24 hours (best visited between 6:00 AM and 8:00 PM). The climb is done on foot from downtown — roughly 250 to 300 steps on the final stretch — and takes 15 to 30 minutes depending on your fitness level.

## Is it worth climbing up to Cerro de la Cruz?

Absolutely yes. It's the highest point in the historic downtown. From the top you'll take in the Pacific Ocean, the city's red-tile rooftops, the Parish of Our Lady of Guadalupe, and the Sierra Madre mountains. It's a free, not-to-be-missed activity for anyone who enjoys photography and sunsets.

## How to get there and which way to climb

The most direct and popular route starts from the Malecón:

1. Walk up Abasolo street heading toward the mountain (climbing away from the sea).
2. Keep going straight, crossing Juárez, Hidalgo and Miramar streets.
3. The slope gradually turns into a series of paved staircases with landings and handrails.

> **Good to know:** the old inclined funicular/elevator is permanently out of service, so the climb has to be done on foot.

## Practical info: hours, cost and difficulty

- **Entry fee:** free.
- **Recommended hours:** open 24/7. The best times are at sunrise (to exercise without the heat) or 45 minutes before sunset.
- **Difficulty level:** moderate to high (requires some cardio effort).
- **Time to complete:** 15 to 30 minutes going up, depending on your pace.

## The sunset from the top

Sunset is the prime time to visit the viewpoint. The sun sets directly over the Pacific Ocean, painting the sky in shades of orange and pink.

**Expert tip:** try to arrive at least 40 minutes before the official sunset time to secure a good spot on the upper platform of the observation tower — it tends to fill up quite a bit.

## Tips for your visit

- **Proper footwear:** wear sneakers or shoes with good grip. Avoid huaraches, sandals or heels.
- **Hydration:** bring your own water bottle — there isn't always a vendor at the top.
- **Sun protection:** if you're going up during the day, wear a hat and biodegradable sunscreen.
- **Safety:** the area is busy and watched over by tourist police during the day and afternoon. Even so, it's best to head down shortly after nightfall.
