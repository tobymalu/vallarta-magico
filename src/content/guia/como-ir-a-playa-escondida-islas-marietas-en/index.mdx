To visit **Hidden Beach** (also known as Playa del Amor) in Marietas Islands, it's **strictly mandatory to book a tour with a federal permit issued by CONANP**. Entry is limited to just 116 people per day, requires swimming through an 80-meter rock tunnel, and children under 10 and adults with mobility issues aren't allowed.

## Why is access to Hidden Beach so limited?

Hidden Beach sits inside a Protected Natural Area and National Park. Due to the environmental impact of mass tourism in past years, Mexico's environmental authorities (CONANP) restricted access to preserve the reef and marine life.

Today, only authorized boats with a special wristband can bring visitors into this part of the park.

## Mandatory requirements and visiting rules

To enter Playa del Amor, you need to follow these current rules:

1. **Book weeks (or months) in advance.** Because of the 116-person daily cap, spots for Hidden Beach fill up fast.
2. **Basic to intermediate swimming level.** The boat stops a few meters from the rock cavity. You'll need to jump in and swim roughly 80 meters, wearing a life jacket and helmet (provided by the operator), to reach the inner beach.
3. **Age restriction.** Children under 10 and older adults with physical limitations or heart conditions aren't allowed in.
4. **Ecological protection.** Sunscreen (even biodegradable) is strictly forbidden before entering the water, and diving fins aren't allowed, to avoid damaging the coral.

## Traditional tour vs. the Hidden Beach tour — what's the difference?

It's very common to confuse the two itineraries. Here are the key differences:

| Feature | Traditional Marietas Tour | Special Hidden Beach Tour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Enters the crater? | ❌ No (viewed from outside only) | ✅ Yes (includes the tunnel swim) |
| Daily visitors | No strict limit in the bay | Limited to 116 people/day |
| Time on the beach | Free time at Nopalera Beach | 15 to 20 minutes inside the crater |
| Best for | Families, kids, party and relaxing | Adventurers and photography lovers |

## Local tips for your visit

- **Ask explicitly before paying:** if your number-one goal is stepping onto the sand inside the crater, confirm that the tour you're quoted includes the Hidden Beach access wristband.
- **Traveling with small kids?** Go with the traditional Marietas Islands tour, which includes Nopalera Beach, snorkeling at outer reefs, kayaking, paddleboarding and a full family vibe on board.
- **Bring something for seasickness:** sailing out to the archipelago in open water can get moderately choppy.
